The mutation needle plot on an experiment's Overview page: one needle per observed mutation, along the axis of one of the reference's sequences, colored by mutation type. It is drawn from the mutations as they stand rather than from anything stored, so it is never out of date with the tables beside it.

The plot shows one sequence at a time, defaulting to the longest — the chromosome, on a reference that also carries plasmids — with a picker for the others. Contigs laid end to end on one axis would need offsets the reader cannot see, and every coordinate would then match nothing in the mutation tables.

This component contributes no page and no sidebar entry. It is a panel on a page aledb-core owns, registered through panel_registry.
